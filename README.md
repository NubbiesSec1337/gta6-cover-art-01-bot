Golden Hour — a GTA-style cover studio. Turn a photo and a caption into multi-panel cover art in the browser, offline. Pick a layout, a colour grade and your photos, crop and position each panel, edit the title, subtitle, tagline and quote, then export a PNG in 1080 x 1080, 1080 x 1350 or 1920 x 1080. Six photorealistic demo portraits and four drawn scenes included; your photos never leave your machine.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Open http://localhost:8080. No network: every file the app loads is under `app/`, and your photos never leave your machine.
